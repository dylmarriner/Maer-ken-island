## 10-LINE SUMMARY

1. Deer: no credible NZ rm beyond 0.33-0.56/yr (unpublished); Forsyth's r=2.33 is an immigration artefact. Forest deer density after heavy hunting ~3.5/km2 (Fiordland); peak reached 20-30 yr after colonisation.
2. Pigs: 12-43/km2 unhunted, 3-8/km2 hunted; 6.2 foetuses/litter; 70% kill recovers in 30 months; max sustained harvest ~20 pigs/km2/yr.
3. Possum: rm 0.22-0.34/yr; 4-8/ha forest average, 25/ha pasture edge; one young per year; 50-70 million nationally.
4. Rodent-mast-stoat: beech mast every 3-5 yr; rat tracking index >40% to <1% across years; mouse "explode", stoats surge months later then switch to birds; rat litter ~10 per 8 weeks.
5. Birds: without predator control forest-bird nest success 27% (+/-15), predation causes 46% of nest failures; small-bird life expectancy ~3 yr vs 12-15; kiwi chicks 5% survive 6 months; kiwi 1-4/km2 under predation, 12 million pre-human (est.).
6. Pastoral: dairy 2.76-2.82 cows/ha, ~1,130 kg MS/ha, 400 kg MS/cow; 1 SU = 550 kg DM; sheep/beef 7-13 SU/ha hill, 8-15 finishing; lambing 120-141%, calving 78-90%; lamb+beef output ~136 kg/ha; adult sheep loss ~4%.
7. Pasture: Waikato 14-17.7 t DM/ha/yr (spring peak 83-87 kg/ha/day, winter ~22-26); Canterbury irrigated up to 21.7 t DM/ha (winter ~8-11).
8. Land cover (2018): exotic grassland 10.6 Mha (~40%), native forest 6.3 Mha (Stats NZ; Te Ara 6.2 Mha = 23%), scrub 2.0 Mha, exotic forest 1.8 Mha; forest was 85-90% pre-human.
9. Carbon: mature native forest ~945 t CO2/ha (~258 t C/ha); MfE tall forest 257.7 t C/ha incl. soil; LUCAS above-ground 158 t C/ha; regenerating 59-94 t C/ha; sequestration ~7 t CO2/ha/yr first 50 yr.
10. Fire: pre-human return 1,000-2,000 yr (lightning 0.1% of modern events); modern ~4,000 wildfires/yr, small mean size ~14 ha, area roughly constant, ~60,000 ha/yr prescribed burns; Maori fire removed ~6.7 Mha forest. Big gaps remain (see GAPS), especially bird densities, invertebrate biomass, tree growth/mortality, and farm feed budgets.
