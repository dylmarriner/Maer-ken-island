Conflict: the experimental runs give about 8-11 kg charcoal per kg of raw bloom (Cleere, Wareham), but 38-39 kg per kg of consolidated billet (Wealden 2015), because Wealden counts preheat and a poorly consolidated bloom. Add charcoal for forging, plus charcoal-making labour (Section 4, charcoal) to get a full cost; no verified person-days-per-kg-of-bloom (Dybowski etc.) was found.
