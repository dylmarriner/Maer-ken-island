Flags/conflicts:
- Acorn gathering 5100-6100 kcal/h (Niclou) is gross gathering only. Published archaeological return-rate tables (Kelly/Simms-type) for processed acorns are far lower, because leaching, cracking and grinding are excluded here. Do not use 6000 kcal/h as an all-in return; the simulation needs a separate processing cost (grinding rate GAP).
- Hadza 85 (youths) vs 370 (child females) vs 1089 (child males) vs 163 (digging roots, kcal/h from g/h) vs 504-656 (Mikea): spread over a factor of 10 due to age, food mix, gross vs net, sample size. Treat tuber digging for adults as a few hundred kcal/h, not thousands (my inference).
- Honey / large game are known to be high-variance and high return per successful encounter in Hadza literature, but I could not verify numeric values.

Flags/conflicts:
- Area per person: 370 m2 (Jeavons claim) vs 700 m2 (FAO, forum) vs 5000 m2 (Pimentel, diverse diet). A 10x+ spread; the first is an advocacy figure with hand labour intensive double digging; kumara needs a 14-yr fallow rotation in Palliser Bay model, so land needed is ~8x the cropped area (COMPUTED from (C+F)/C = 16/2).

- COMPUTED: at 10.15 t/ha, 10% storage loss, 1.10 t/ha seed, net ~8.1 t/ha; at 1.14 kcal/g that is ~9.2 million kcal/ha, i.e. ~25,000 kcal/day/ha; a 2000 kcal/person/day full-kumara diet needs ~0.08 ha cropped per person per year (excludes fallow).
