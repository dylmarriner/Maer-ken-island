## Summary (10 lines)
1. VERIFIED, ready to use: RothC k values (DPM 10, RPM 0.3, BIO 0.66, HUM 0.02 /yr), its temperature function, vegetation cover factor 0.6, DPM/RPM by vegetation, and BIO/HUM 46:54 split.
2. The brief's turnover times (DPM 0.165 yr) do not match the RothC document; the document gives k=10 /yr (0.1 yr). Use the document.
3. Q10 = 2 appears in LIDET fine-root decay (Gholz 2000) and in MOD17 for all biomes; litter k range 0.18 (tundra) to 1.3 /yr (rain forest) from Zhang 2008.
4. Ecosystem carbon turnover mean 23 yr, equator 15, high north 255 (Carvalhais 2014); soil C in models is NPP x turnover (Todd-Brown 2013).
5. NZ soil C: about 100 t C/ha at 0-30 cm in agricultural soil; forest-to-pasture adds 13.7 t C/ha; drained organic soils lose 2.9 t C/ha/yr.
6. Nutrients: N response ratio 1.19 temperate forest and 1.53 temperate grassland (LeBauer & Treseder); N and P act synergistically (Elser). NZ pasture N response is 10-20 kg DM/kg N in spring, 4-8 in winter; Olsen P targets 20-30 (ash, sedimentary) and 35-45 (pumice, peat).
7. Seasonal pasture growth tables (Waikato 17.7 t/yr, trough 22-26 in Jun-Jul, peak 83-87 in Oct-Nov; Canterbury trough 5-8) give a ready monthly multiplier; MOD17 LUE 0.96 (needleleaf) to 1.28 (closed shrub) g C/MJ and NPP/GPP 0.47 close the seasonal GPP-NPP chain.
8. Food and seed: bracken rhizome stock 2.92 kg/m2 dry, 10-30% starch, only deep fertile soils yield edible roots, recovery takes about 20 yr; mast follows the temperature-difference cue (delta T) with low variability for frugivore-dispersed species.
9. Succession and disturbance: kanuka height 34.4(1-0.993^age) m; soil depth 20 cm at 10 yr to 58 cm at 70 yr; scar soil accrual 3.5 then 1.2 mm/yr; grazing stalls conversion to forest.
10. Gaps (NOT FOUND): fire danger indices, windthrow, wetland extent and peat accumulation, harakeke yield, NZ fruit and kūmara yields, LAI by biome, seed dispersal distances, and the Olson 1963 and Lloyd & Taylor 1994 numbers. Search budget ran out; these need a second pass.
