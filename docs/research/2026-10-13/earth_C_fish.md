1. Best sourced NPP-to-fish template: Stock et al. 2017, C = alpha x NPP x TE^(TLeq-1), TE = 0.1, TLeq 3 to >4, wet weight to C ratio 1/9; typical catches 0.01-1 g C m-2 yr-1 (about 0.09-9 t wet weight km-2 yr-1), within factor 2.
