# Session log: 2026-10-13

The owner's goal for the session was **"get 100% production ready"**, refined during it to:
*everything replicates Earth life and how humans function, think, feel, sense, behave, invent
and create; buildings and resources really built, gathered and carried.* This records what was
learned, what changed, and what is still open. The commit messages and PR descriptions carry the
detail; this is the map.

## Owner decisions made this session

- **No Docker.** The backend runs as a binary (systemd or by hand); frontends (web dashboard,
  `island-ui`) run on other devices and talk to it over HTTP. `deploy/Dockerfile` is kept
  building but is not the supported route.
- **Gates 1, 2 and 3 are closed.** The island, its weather and the estate plan are the ones the
  project was built on, and that is the approval. The island is no longer provisional.
- **Don't ask for approvals.** For a preference call, pick a sensible default, record it where it
  can be overturned, and carry on. Only stop for what truly needs the owner (their GitHub
  settings, asset provenance only they know, upstream PRs under their name).
- **Everything replicates Earth.** Every action should take its real time; work should cost real
  energy and real materials; senses, mind and behaviour should be human.

## Production readiness

### Found and fixed (PR #8, merged)

- **An installed backend could not start.** `validation::default_reference_dir()` was
  `CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR/../../fixtures/reference`, a path compiled in from the build machine, and
  `IslandLife` loads its labour tables from it. Anywhere but the build tree the island refused
  with `MissingReference`. It now reads `ISLAND_REFERENCE_DIR`, then the build tree, then
  `./fixtures/reference`.
- **The canon was not found from an installed scenario.** `canon_for` tried the scenario's
  repo-relative path from the working directory and beside the scenario; it now also looks from
  every folder above the scenario.
- **Packaging.** `deploy/build-release.sh` ships `fixtures/island` and `fixtures/reference` in
  `dist/share/island/`; `deploy/island.service` reads them from `/usr/local/share/island/`.
  The Dockerfile now copies the fixtures it compiles in (`include_str!` of `fixtures/human/*`)
  and has a `.dockerignore` (the 13 GB `target/` was being sent as build context).
- **A restart threw the island away.** `island serve` always built a new island. Now
  `--autosave MIN` keeps `<snapshot-dir>/latest.mks` (written beside and renamed, so a crash
  mid-save keeps the old one; a failed step does not overwrite the last good save) and
  `--resume` carries on from it.
- **SIGTERM was not handled.** The server waited for Ctrl-C only, so `systemctl stop` killed the
  process mid-step. It now stops on either and waits for the step and the save.
  The unit uses `--autosave 30 --resume` and `TimeoutStopSec=300` (a save took 74.6 s here).
- **CI could not see any of this**, because every job runs inside the source tree. New job
  **installed backend**: builds `dist/`, installs it outside the repository, hides the
  repository's fixtures, waits for the island to answer with a clock.

Verified by hand: an empty pack folder refuses with `MissingReference`; the shipped packs serve
after about 45 s; SIGTERM at tick 119 saved tick 120 and exited; a restart with `--resume`
carried on.

### Open (PR #11)

- `/api/health` answered "ok" about a frozen island (a failed step stops the sim thread while the
  server keeps answering). It now answers **503** `island stopped`, so a monitor restarts it and
  `--resume` picks up the last good save.

## The realism research

Three research passes compared the code with the real world, with sources, in
`docs/research/2026-10-13/` (`humans.md`, `work.md`, `earth.md` and their raw source notes).
`WORK_REMAINING.md` section 8 turns them into the ordered programme **R0-R10**.

- **Humans.** The body is realistic and tested (metabolism, water, sleep, growth, gestation,
  ageing). The mind barely drives behaviour: `choose_action` (`humans/autonomy.rs`) is an
  argmax over needs and a few cell averages. Senses, memory, emotion, learning, culture and
  language are computed every tick and nothing reads them to act. There is no smell or taste;
  pain is built from hygiene, fatigue and hydration, so injury does not hurt; skill never
  improves with practice; "invention" is a timer that unlocks recipes at fixed thresholds;
  cognition, culture and language are rebuilt from the profile every tick. None of these gaps
  had a deviation row.
- **Work.** Most actions do nothing on the island: the resource economy starts empty and the
  founders hold no tools or containers, so Gather, Mine, Craft and Build fail; only felling and
  hauling run (`life.rs` `work`). The realistic labour models in `labour.rs` (dig, mine, pan,
  build a timber frame) were called only by tests. Food and water were taken from kilometres
  away the instant someone ate or drank; carrying had no mass except for logs; buildings appear
  free.
- **The living world.** One plant-carbon field and one assumed grazer pool. NPP is set at
  bootstrap and ignores season and soil; people eat whole-cell biomass with no edible fraction;
  litterfall, grazer faeces and carcasses left the books; nothing decomposed. Grazing eats 5% of
  production everywhere, where managed New Zealand pasture loses 70-90%.
- **Corrections** the research made to earlier assumptions: NZ has no default 40% B0 fishing
  target (limits are 20% soft, 10% hard); Klein 2007 gives 87 crops and 35% of production, not
  "75%"; RothC's DPM decays at k = 10/yr, not 0.165 yr; NZ kelp NPP is 374-615 gC/m²/yr.

## What the programme has done so far

- **R1, water at home (PR #10, merged; D42).** A rainwater tank on the house roof (188 m² on the
  default island). Inflow `(24-hour rain - 1 mm) × roof` (Harrison Grierson for Greater
  Wellington, 2011); household use 278 L per person per day besides drinking, cut to the 20 L
  emergency ration under a day's supply (ESR 2021); used water to the soil. Sized from the
  estate's own climatology by sequent-peak and installed as whole 30,000 L tanks (BRANZ Level),
  at least one: the default island gets one. Founders at home drink from it.
- **R1b, fetching by hand (PR #12, open).** With the tank dry, a founder walks from the house to
  the nearest water at the packs' pace, drinks there, and carries water home in the shed's two
  20 L jerrycans (IFRC family size, 1 kg empty) within their load limit. Measured: a 40-minute
  round trip, 30.4 L carried home.
- **R0, soil carbon (in progress).** Litterfall, grazer faeces (as farmyard manure) and carcasses
  feed four RothC compartments under every land cell, decomposing at RothC's rates with its
  temperature and cover modifiers and clay partition (Coleman, Prout & Milne 2024). Moisture
  modifier 1, Rothamsted's 23.4% clay, year-average temperature factor, no IOM pool: deviation
  D43 to be written.

## Things learned the hard way

- **The upstream needs model hydrates a person when they choose to drink**, before any water
  reaches them, so they stop choosing to drink within minutes. A water trip that only advanced
  while the founder kept choosing to drink stranded them halfway. Trips now run to the end once
  started; the real fix (tell the mind it is walking, hydrate only on actual drinking) is part of
  R6.
- **A medium cell is about 2 km across.** "Water in my cell" is still a walk from the house, so
  at home the walk is measured from the house's position, not the cell.
- **Monthly means hide dry spells.** Sizing a tank from monthly climatology gives 0 L in a
  climate wet every month; the one-tank floor is what a real house has.
- **Search summaries are not sources.** ESR's "store 7 days" turned out to be emergency advice,
  not tank sizing, and became the rationing figure instead. Every pack figure this session was
  re-read in its source.
- **Fetching sources:** `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"` plus `pdftotext -layout` works for ESR,
  Greater Wellington, Rothamsted, BRANZ Level; Auckland Council, Central Otago DC and NCBI/PMC
  block automated fetching; Firecrawl credits are exhausted.
- **Tooling:** `cargo test --test T --lib name` applies the filter to every binary, so an
  integration test can silently run 0 tests; `cargo test` takes one filter; `pkill -f` on a
  pattern inside the running command kills that shell; `gh pr edit` fails on the Projects
  (classic) deprecation, so use `gh api`; don't switch branches while cargo runs in the same
  checkout.
